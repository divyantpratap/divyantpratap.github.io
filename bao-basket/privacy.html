Privacy Policy — Bao Basket: Dumpling Merge

Effective date: 3 October 2026
Developer: Siya Tech Venture (Divyant Pratap), divyantpratap@gmail.com

Bao Basket: Dumpling Merge is a single-player merge puzzle game. We built it to be private by design.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps your best scores, your unfinished match, the difficulty mode you last chose and your sound on/off setting in the app's local storage on your device, so it can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: used for the small haptic buzz when dumplings merge. It is not used for anything else.
  • The app does not request the Internet permission and cannot make network requests.

Children

The game contains no advertising, purchases, chat or external links, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date changed.
